LEGOKneesall

A brick-built model of Kneesall, Nottinghamshire, laid out from open data: the real roads, buildings measured from the Environment Agency's laser survey, churchyard, hedges and streams, with St Bartholomew's Church, the Tudor Old Hall, the Rajdhaani (the old Angel Inn) and the school, surrounded by grass paddocks and the crops the Crop Map of England recorded in 2024: oats, wheat, barley, beans and maize, as they look this month (Y for the next).
